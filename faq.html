Frequently Asked Questions

ARE YOUR PIECES REAL STERLING SILVER?

Yes — every piece is solid 925 sterling silver, hallmarked and stamped. Nothing is plated brass or alloy core.

WHAT IS MOISSANITE?

Moissanite is a certified gemstone with brilliance higher than diamond, ethically grown and graded for every set stone above 4mm.

HOW LONG DOES DELIVERY TAKE?

Orders placed before 12:00 PM arrive in Dubai and Abu Dhabi within 1–2 business days, or 2–4 business days if placed after 12:00 PM. The northern emirates take 2–4 business days. Delivery is free on orders over 899 AED and a flat 25 AED below that — full tracking is sent to your email and phone as soon as your parcel leaves our studio. See Shipping & Delivery for details.

CAN I RETURN OR EXCHANGE?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery, provided the jewellery is unworn, undamaged and in its original packaging; return shipping for change-of-mind returns is the customer’s responsibility. Exchanges are welcome subject to availability, with shipping charges applying. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method after inspection. Personalised and custom-made pieces cannot be returned or exchanged. See Returns & Care for the full policy.

WILL TARNISH BE A PROBLEM?

Not with basic care. Wear your silver often, keep it dry, keep it away from perfumes and chlorine, and give it a wipe with the polishing cloth after wear. For deeper cleaning, warm water with a drop of mild soap and a soft brush, then dry thoroughly.

DO YOU DO CUSTOM DESIGNS?

Yes. Custom designs are on request — from stack sets to one-off pieces. Contact us to begin a commission.

HOW DO I FIND MY SIZE?

Use the Sizing Guide with the paper-strip method and the mm chart (US sizes 5–9), measuring your finger at the end of the day. Between sizes? Choose the larger size for stack comfort — or contact us for assistance with your ring size.

IS MY CHECKOUT SECURE?

Payments are processed over encrypted, PCI-compliant gateways. We never store card details.
