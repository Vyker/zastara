Our Story

Made to Be Stacked. Made to Be Yours.

ZASTARA began with a simple love for jewellery and the endless possibilities of styling it.

The idea was born from a personal appreciation for delicate, beautifully made pieces that could be worn every day, mixed effortlessly and styled in different ways. Jewellery that feels just as special worn alone as it does layered together.

We wanted to create a destination for those who share that love. A place to discover timeless pieces without compromising on quality, beauty or individuality.

Our collections bring together 925 sterling silver and brilliant moissanite, carefully selected for their elegance, versatility and lasting appeal. From understated everyday essentials to eye-catching statement pieces, each design is chosen with the intention of complementing the next.

At the heart of ZASTARA is the belief that jewellery should reflect the person wearing it.

Some days, it's a single delicate necklace. Other days, it's a perfectly imperfect stack of rings, layered necklaces or a curated collection of earrings. The beauty lies in making it your own.

We believe beautiful jewellery should feel special without being reserved for special occasions.

No rules. No set combinations. Just pieces you love, worn your way.
