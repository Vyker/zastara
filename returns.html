Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love every piece you choose from ZASTARA. If something isn't quite right, we're here to help.

Returns

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery, provided your jewellery is unworn, undamaged and returned in its original packaging.

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer for change of mind returns.

Exchanges

Need a different size? We're happy to assist with exchanges, subject to availability. Shipping charges apply.

Refunds

Once your return has been received and inspected, approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method. Processing times may vary depending on your payment provider.

Personalised Pieces

Personalised and custom-made jewellery cannot be returned or exchanged for change of mind.

Faulty or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, please contact us so we can make it right. We'll arrange a suitable resolution and cover applicable return shipping costs in accordance with UAE consumer protection laws.

For return or exchange enquiries, please contact our customer care team.
