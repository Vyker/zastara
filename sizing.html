Sizing Guide

How to Measure

Ring sizes run US 5–9. For the truest fit, measure your finger at the end of the day, when it is at its largest, at room temperature.

  • Wrap a strip of paper or non-stretch string snugly around the base of your finger.
  • Mark where it overlaps and measure the length in millimetres — that is your finger circumference.
  • Match the circumference to the chart below. Between sizes? Choose the larger size for stack comfort.
  • Knuckles wider than the finger base? Measure at the knuckle and pick the size that slides over comfortably.
